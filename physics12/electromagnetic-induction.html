2. Physics Logic

ε = -N × (ΔΦ / Δt)
ε: Induced EMF (Volts)
N: Number of loops
ΔΦ/Δt: Rate of magnetic flux change
Lenz's Law: The negative sign indicates the direction of induced current opposes the change in flux.
