Molecular Assembly SOP

Phase 1: Transcription (Nucleus)
Build the mRNA strand by pairing RNA nucleotides to the DNA template. Remember: RNA uses Uracil (U) instead of Thymine (T).
Phase 2: Translation (Ribosome)
Read the mRNA codons (groups of 3 bases) inside the Ribosome. Select the correct tRNA to deliver the specific Amino Acid.
The Genetic Code: DNA → mRNA → Protein. This directional flow of genetic information is the foundation of all cellular life.
