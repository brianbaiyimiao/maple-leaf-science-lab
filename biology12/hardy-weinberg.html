Evolutionary Forces (Disruptors)

q (a) = 0.50
If < 1.0, individuals with 'aa' trait are hunted/die before reproducing. Equilibrium is broken.
Small populations cause random fluctuations in allele frequencies (Bottleneck/Founder effect).
Theorem: Evolution occurs ONLY when allele frequencies change. If fitness = 1.0 and pop is large, p and q will remain perfectly flat (HWE).
