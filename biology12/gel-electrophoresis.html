Lab 2.1: Forensic DNA Fingerprinting

Maple Leaf Biological Sciences

Standard Operating Procedure

Step 1: Prepare Buffer
Pour TAE buffer into the electrophoresis chamber to conduct electricity.
Step 2: Micropipette Samples
Carefully load digested DNA samples mixed with loading dye into the agarose gel wells.
Step 3: Run Electrophoresis
Apply 120V. Negatively charged DNA will migrate towards the positive anode (+). Wait until the dye front reaches the bottom.
Step 4: UV Analysis
The DNA was stained with Ethidium Bromide. Expose to UV light to visualize the bands.
Step 5: Forensic Report
Based on the banding pattern matching the Crime Scene DNA, who is the culprit?
