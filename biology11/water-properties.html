Welcome to the Water Lab!

Water (H₂O) is a polar molecule. The oxygen end has a slight negative charge (δ-), and the hydrogen ends have a slight positive charge (δ+).

This polarity allows water molecules to form Hydrogen Bonds with each other and with other substances, giving water unique properties essential for life.
