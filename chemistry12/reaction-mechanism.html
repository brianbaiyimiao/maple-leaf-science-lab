Maple Leaf Chemistry 12

Reaction Mechanism Playback

1: NO₂ + NO₂ → NO₃ + NO SLOW (RDS)
2: NO₃ + CO → NO₂ + CO₂ FAST
Net: NO₂ + CO → NO + CO₂
Analysis:
1. Intermediate: NO₃. It is produced in Step 1 and consumed in Step 2. It sits in the "valley" between the two peaks.

2. Rate-Determining Step (RDS): Step 1. It has a significantly higher Activation Energy (Ea₁) hill, controlling the overall speed of the reaction.
