Experiment: Projectile Motion

Objective (实验目的)

To analyze the motion of a projectile in 2 dimensions and verify that horizontal and vertical motions are independent.

Procedure (实验步骤)

  1. Set Initial Velocity (v₀) to 30 m/s.
  2. Set Launch Angle (θ) to 45°.
  3. Click LAUNCH and observe the path.
  4. Switch to the "Data Table" tab.
  5. Record the final time and distance (Range).

Analysis Questions (分析问题)

  • Does the horizontal velocity (vx) change over time? Why?
  • At what time does the vertical velocity (vy) become zero? (Max height)
  • Calculate the theoretical range using:
    R = (v₀² * sin(2θ)) / g
