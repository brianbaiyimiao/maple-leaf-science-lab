Lab: The Atwood Machine

Objective

Investigate the relationship between total mass, net force, and acceleration in a connected system.

Theory

$F_{net} = m_{hang}g - f_{friction}$
$a = \frac{F_{net}}{M_{cart} + m_{hang}}$
$T = M_{cart}a + f_{friction}$

Procedure

  1. Start with friction $\mu = 0$.
  2. Increase hanging mass. Observe how acceleration increases.
  3. Note that Tension $T$ is NOT equal to the weight of the hanging mass ($mg$).
  4. Add friction and repeat.
