Lab: Incline Pulley

Objective

Analyze a system of connected objects where one is on an incline and the other hangs vertically.

Free Body Diagram (M1)

  • Gravity (Purple): Straight down.
  • Normal (Blue): Perpendicular to ramp.
  • Tension (Pink): Up the ramp.
  • Friction (Orange): Opposes motion.

Procedure

  1. Set $M_1$ and $\theta$.
  2. Predict: Will adding more $m_2$ pull $M_1$ up the ramp?
  3. Start and observe acceleration.
