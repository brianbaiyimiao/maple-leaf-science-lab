Lab: Multi-Body Momentum

1. Collision (Inelastic)

Simulates N objects colliding at the center and sticking together.

Formula: vfinal = (Σ mivi) / Σ mi

2. Explosion

An object breaks into N fragments. You enter data for N-1 fragments, the system calculates the last one.

Formula: Pinitial = Σ Pfinal

3. Fireworks Mode

The object launches as a projectile. The velocity at the time of explosion (vtop) becomes the initial momentum for the fragments.

Physics Note: Angles are standard (0° = Right, 90° = Up).
