Lab: Newton's Laws

1. Law of Inertia

Set Friction = 0. Push the sled, then set Thrust to 0. Does it stop? (No force = No change in motion)

2. F=ma Investigation

Keep mass constant (e.g. 50kg). Increase Thrust. Record Acceleration. Is the relationship linear?

3. Action-Reaction

Enable "Show Action-Reaction". Notice that the force pushing the sled forward is equal and opposite to the force pushing the air backward.
