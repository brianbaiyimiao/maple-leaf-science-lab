Lab: 2D Static Equilibrium

Objective

Analyze how the angles of suspension cables affect the tension required to support a hanging mass.

Theory (Newton's 1st Law)

Since the sign is stationary ($\Sigma F = 0$):

  • X-Axis: Left Pull = Right Pull
    $T_1 \cos\theta_1 = T_2 \cos\theta_2$
  • Y-Axis: Up Pull = Gravity
    $T_1 \sin\theta_1 + T_2 \sin\theta_2 = mg$

Critical Thinking

What happens when the angle approaches 0° (horizontal)? Why can't we pull the strings perfectly straight?
